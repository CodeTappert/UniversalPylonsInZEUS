# Changelog

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-08

### Added

- Eden Editor support: the aircraft's Pylons attributes list every pylon weapon (non-native ones in orange). Forced weapons are saved with the mission and applied in the editor and at mission start.

### Changed

- Renamed to "Universal Pylons (Zeus & Eden)"; new Workshop preview image.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-07

### Added

- Launcher logo and mod info (GitHub link) in the Arma 3 Launcher.
- Steam Workshop release.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-07

### Added

- Every pylon weapon in the game is listed in ZEN's Zeus pylon menu; non-native weapons are shown in orange.
- Loadouts are forced onto the aircraft, so non-native weapons stick.
